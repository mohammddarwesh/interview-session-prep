## Full Question Bank

### Traditional HR Questions

2. Why do you want to work for our company?
3. What are your greatest strengths and weaknesses?
4. Why are you looking for a change?
5. Tell me about the gap in your resume.

8. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

10. How do you deal with criticism?

### Behavioural HR Questions (STAR)
11. Tell me about a time when you were not satisfied with your performance.
12. Tell me about a time when you were made to work under close supervision.
13. Can you tell me about a time where you were happy with your work?
14. Tell me about a time where you experienced difficulty at work while working on a project.
15. Tell me about a time where you displayed leadership skills.
16. Was there any point in your career where you made any mistake?
17. How did you handle disagreements with your manager?
18. Tell me how you will handle it if suddenly the priorities of a project were changed.

### Opinion-Based HR Questions
19. You win a million-dollar lottery. Would you still be working?
20. What would you do if you were working under a bad boss?
21. What do you think is an ideal work environment?
22. What does motivation mean to you?
23. What is your dream company like?
24. What do you do to ensure a certain number of tasks is completed effectively?
25. What would you prefer — being liked or being feared?
26. How long do you think you will be working for us if you are hired?
27. If you were reborn as an animal, what animal would you want to be?
28. Will you lie for the company under any circumstances?

### Brainteaser HR Questions
29. Being perfect and delivering late vs being good and delivering on time?
30. Judy's mother had 4 children... what was the name of the fourth child?
31. How many times a day do the clock's hands overlap?
32. You have two vessels of 3l and 5l. How do you get 4l of water?

### Salary Related Questions
33. What is your current salary?
34. What is your salary expectation?
35. How much do you think you should be paid?

- What's the tech stack and are there plans to evolve it?

## Answer Templates

### Tell Me About Yourself
**Structure:** Present → Past → Future
- Present: who you are, what you do now
- Past: 1-2 key achievements with numbers/proof
- Future: what role/company you're looking for

### STAR Method
- **S**ituation: context
- **T**ask: what you needed to do
- **A**ction: what YOU did (use "I", not "we")
- **R**esult: outcome with numbers if possible

### Strengths Formula
- Claim + Example (numerical proof) + Impact

### Why This Company?
- Their mission + your skills matching + growth opportunity

### Weakness Formula
- Real weakness + what you're doing to fix it
- Avoid clichés (perfectionism, "I work too hard")
